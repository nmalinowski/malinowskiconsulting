EXHIBIT 02

Services

Eight items, in order

01

AI Readiness Assessment

We assess your AI readiness, find the use cases that fit health care realities, and rank them by clinical value and risk. You get a sequenced plan, not a slide deck.

02

Vendor Evaluation and Technology Selection

We evaluate AI vendors and tools against your requirements for interoperability, compliance, and long-term upkeep. Build versus buy, decided on evidence.

03

Governance Frameworks and Policies

We write the governance frameworks and policies your AI use runs on: accountability, bias review, transparency, and ongoing audit. Built to pass review.

04

AI-SDLC Implementation Guidance

We guide your teams on AI-assisted development: how to build, test, validate, and ship AI features inside a regulated environment, with the governance to match. Your engineers do the building. We set the method.

05

Change Management and Adoption

We guide teams through adoption with training and rollout plans that stick, so the roadmap survives contact with daily work.

06

Performance and Ongoing Advisory

We define the measures that show whether AI is working, then stay on as advisors while you run it. Monthly retainer, scoped to what you need.

07

Vibe Code Cleanup

AI-generated code ships fast and breaks later: duplicated logic, missing tests, security gaps, and code no one fully owns or can explain. We audit the codebase, flag risk and dead code, and give your team a cleanup plan in priority order: what to fix, refactor, test, or retire. You get code your team can maintain and your auditors can follow.

08

AISDLC Training

Standalone training on the AI software development lifecycle for engineering teams and the leaders who oversee them. We teach the full AISDLC method: planning and specifying work for AI coding tools and agents, building with agent harnesses, testing and validating AI-generated code, code review, and shipping inside your governance rules in a regulated environment. Hands-on and built around your codebase and workflows, not generic slides, so your team leaves able to run AISDLC on its own.

EXHIBIT 07

Questions on file

FAQ

Q What industries do you work in?

Health care first, and other regulated industries where compliance, data privacy, and AI governance are non-negotiable.

Q Do you work with our internal team or replace it?

We work with your team. Your engineers and clinicians keep ownership of what ships. We advise, assess, and set governance. We do not take over delivery.

Q How do you handle HIPAA and protected health information?

Data sovereignty by design. PHI stays under your control. We assess readiness and set governance within HIPAA requirements, with frameworks for accountability, transparency, and ongoing audit, so your AI use stands up to review.

Q What is vibe code cleanup?

It is an audit and cleanup plan for code that was built quickly with AI tools. We review the codebase for security gaps, duplicated logic, missing tests, and code no one can explain, then rank what to fix, refactor, test, or retire. Your team does the cleanup with a clear order of work.

Q What is AISDLC training?

AISDLC training is a standalone service that teaches your engineering team the AI software development lifecycle: specifying work for AI coding tools and agents, building with agent harnesses, and testing, validating, and reviewing AI-generated code before it ships. It is hands-on, built around your codebase and governance rules, and separate from implementation guidance. Your team runs the method on its own after training.
