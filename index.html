Frequently Asked Questions

Do you implement the software yourselves?
No, we are pure strategic advisors. This keeps us vendor-agnostic and focused entirely on your outcomes, not billing implementation hours.
What industries do you specialize in?
Healthcare, Pharma, and Finance, industries where compliance, data privacy, and ethical AI are non-negotiable. We also audit web properties in other regulated categories.
How do you handle data privacy (HIPAA)?
We design architectures that prioritize data sovereignty: on-prem, private cloud, and model choices that ensure PHI never leaves your control.
What is the typical engagement timeline?
Strategic roadmaps typically take 4–6 weeks. Ongoing advisory and governance oversight can be structured as a monthly retainer.
