Who this is for

If your team shipped fast with AI tools and now the codebase feels fragile, flaky tests, hidden shortcuts, unclear data handling, I'll help you get clarity before you invest more.

  • Founders and product teams who built an MVP with AI assistance
  • Engineering leads inheriting a vibe-coded repo that needs hardening
  • Healthcare and regulated teams where compliance and reliability matter

The audit process

  1. You share context, repo access, goals, and constraints. I sign an NDA if needed and timebox the read-through.
  2. I audit manually, architecture, security, data flow, and maintainability. I note what's solid, what's risky, and what to fix first.
  3. You get a fixed-price quote, clear scope, timeline, and options. We decide together what to ship; you keep the audit either way.

What “clean” looks like

Clean isn't a rewrite, it's a repo your team can extend without surprises. That usually means tighter boundaries, documented decisions, and tests around the riskiest paths.

  • Reliable builds with lint, type-check, and test coverage that actually gates merges
  • Clear module boundaries so new features don't ripple across the codebase
  • Explicit data handling and auth checks at integration points

FAQ

How long does the audit take?

Small repos: 2–3 days. Larger systems: about a week. I'll give you a date range up front and keep you posted if scope shifts.

Do you rewrite the whole app?

No. I target the highest-risk areas first and leave solid parts alone. You'll get a prioritized list so you can ship improvements incrementally.

What do you need to start?

Repo access, a short intake call, and any compliance constraints (HIPAA, SOC 2, etc.). I can work with GitHub, GitLab, or a zip snapshot.

What if we want to pause after the audit?

Totally fine. The audit deliverable is yours, findings, risks, and a fixed-price plan, whether or not we continue to the clean-up.
