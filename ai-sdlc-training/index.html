FAQ

Can we mix tracks?

Yes. Many teams start with the team workshop and give individuals the self-paced modules as follow-on. We'll tailor the plan.

Is this tool-specific?

No. I demo with current models and editors, but the workflow transfers. We'll map the patterns to your stack.

How is this different from a video course?

We work on your backlog items in labs, not synthetic exercises. You leave with artifacts from your own system.

Do you offer in-person delivery?

Optional. Virtual is the default; we can add an on-site day for kickoff or a capstone review where it helps.
